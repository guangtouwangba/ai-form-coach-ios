# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility and focusing on what is best for the community.
Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, publishing others' private information, and discriminatory or
derogatory comments.

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Instances may be reported privately through the repository's security or
maintainer contact features. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
